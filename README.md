# Tamago

A fluffy egg plush you can pet in your browser. Stroke it, spin it around, hug it, groom it, give it a bath, and tuck it into its egg carton.

Play: open the GitHub Pages link for this repository.

- Drag on the plush to stroke it. Drag beside it to turn it.
- Tap to boop it. Hold to squish it.
- The grooming kit is on the left (keys 1â€“6). The heart button is a hug.
- Pick a room from the top-left button.
- Daily pets are saved in your own browser.

Made with three.js. All sounds are synthesized in the browser.
